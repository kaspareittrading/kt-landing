The chessboard

Your capital,
sorted by risk

Not all money works equally hard. We place it like pieces on a chessboard: up front are the ones that attack, in the back the ones that hold. That way an offensive level can have a weak phase without putting the core at risk.

♚ ♛
Conservative

King and queen

The core. Low risk, built for capital preservation. Especially suited for larger accounts and for prop trading and funded accounts, where a loss limit must not be breached.

♜ ♞ ♝
Moderate

Rook, knight and bishop

The middle ground. A balanced ratio of risk and return. These levels provide steady growth without the swings becoming uncomfortable.

♟ ♟ ♟
Offensive

The pawns

The attackers. They run with offensive risk and generate the cash flow. Many small units, some of which may fall without the position collapsing.

Profits flow from the bottom up. What the offensive levels earn strengthens the conservative ones. The core stays protected.

Test first,
then deliver

Most providers show a nice equity curve and say: it works. For us, that is where the work begins.

  1. 1
    Edge analysis

    First the market mechanics have to make sense. Without a statistically provable edge, no strategy moves on.

  2. 2
    Purchased price data

    High-quality data sets covering 15 to 25 years instead of standard broker data.

  3. 3
    Walk-Forward analysis

    Optimized on one period, tested on months the strategy has never seen.

  4. 4
    Monte Carlo simulation

    Thousands of runs show which drawdown is realistically possible.

  5. 5
    Ongoing monitoring

    Live versus simulation. If the values diverge, the portfolio is reweighted.

  6. 6
    Our own backtesting engine

    Standard programs were not enough, so we built our own software.

  7. 7
    Final test

    Finally, several years of completely unseen data. Only then is it delivered.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask most

How much time do I need to invest each day?
Very little. One or two quick checks a day are enough. The systems trade on their own, you don't interfere with running trades.
Do I need prior knowledge or programming skills?
No. You drag the portfolio onto a chart, instrument and timeframe don't matter. There are setup guides from A to Z as video and PDF, plus community and support. If you want it completely effortless, you can book the setup service for €99.
How much capital do I need?
For a portfolio we recommend €2,000 to €5,000, so that the diversification inside can actually take effect. Single strategies are possible from about €500, provided your broker supports micro lots. The planned futures portfolios will require more.
Which platform and which broker do I need?
For the portfolios and core strategies you need MetaTrader 5, and you choose the broker freely. MetaTrader 4 is not supported. For stock trading with All Time High you need an account with Interactive Brokers, also via CapTrader or LYNX.
Does my computer have to run all the time?
The software has to be running for trades to be placed. You can do that on your own PC, but a Windows VPS for around €10 to €30 per month is more convenient. That way everything runs around the clock, even when your computer is off.
Are the portfolios suitable for prop challenges?
Yes. Every strategy portfolio comes with a prop version with news filter and Friday close. With the free Prop Challenge simulator you can check in advance how likely you are to pass before you put any money in.
On how many accounts can I use it?
One license allows up to 10 private and up to 10 prop accounts, all in your name. The account numbers can be swapped at any time. Other people need their own membership.
Does the membership renew automatically?
Yes, for twelve months at a time, and you can cancel anytime. As long as your subscription runs without interruption, you keep your entry price permanently, even if prices for new customers go up.
Do you manage my money?
No. We do not provide portfolio management or asset management within the meaning of MiFID II. You get software, install it yourself on your own account and keep full control. We have no access to your account, login details or capital.
Will this still work in a few years?
That's why the quality process keeps running, even after launch. We continuously compare live behavior with the simulation and reweight when the values diverge. New strategies and portfolios are added regularly and are included in Premium at no extra charge.
